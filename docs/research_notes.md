# Research notes

The methodological result is an inspectable pipeline, not proof of ecological error. See `reports/results.md` for actual counts and ten source-linked review examples.

- Schema evolution breaks naive concatenation: the same workbook includes density-derived fractional totals, individual records, repeat/auxiliary samples and duplicate representations.
- Identifier continuity is contextual. Historic's shared source row supports identity, but the 2002 report explicitly invalidates that inference on 71G. Duplicate IDs also restrict which temporal pairs are evaluated.
- Missingness has collection semantics. Discontinued browning and absent continuous 1941 heights must not look like ordinary source blanks.
- Temporal rules can flag nonnegative height and arm measurements that scalar validity checks accept. Breakage and standing-dead measurements make these review candidates, not automatically errors.
- Spatial coverage is part of the result. Local-coordinate maps and shared undated locations prevent several tempting comparisons. No movement result is supported by the current inputs.
- Every result can identify its source hash, worksheet/layer, physical row/feature, field and transformation. This is a reproducibility mechanism, not a guarantee of perpetual upstream availability.

Potential conference claims must be bounded by the generated results: conservative harmonization of actual heterogeneous sources; documented identity exclusions; additional temporal review candidates; executable field lineage; and explicit accounting for spatial checks that cannot be applied. Conflicting explicit dead/alive records are semantic contradictions requiring review, not evidence of a literal biological reversal.

Context-aware validation detects not only malformed data, but also transitions whose interpretation depends on scientific context. Structurally valid height/arm decreases may be real breakage; after-death heights may describe standing dead cacti. Unusual growth is a statistical screen. These categories must remain separate from integrity breaches and confirmed source errors.

The executed [temporal interpretation](../reports/temporal_interpretation.md) quantifies note/Verify coverage and source-checks L130 and L167. Context counts are counts of finding pairs, not independent notes, biological explanations or error rates. L130's live note accompanies the measured height/arm decrease; L167's later positive heights have unknown status after an explicit dead note. Shared-row Verify No/Yes cannot adjudicate either history. Descriptive free text remains available without being promoted to formal status.

Synthetic benchmark interventions now carry scenario_semantics. Plausible biological loss, semantic contradictions, statistical anomalies and integrity violations are reported separately. Detector recovery establishes screening/consistency capabilities under the specified intervention, not that the simulated biological values are invalid.
